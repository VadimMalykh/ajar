Closing Time: three looks for the department store

The store still wears grey brick from stockroom to roof. Below are three looks for the whole of it, each built into the level and photographed from the same 66 cameras as the level is now. A look changes textures only: no wall, floor or railing moves, and the level plays the same.

Picks can be mixed, for example the atrium from one look and the departments from another. Your picks are collected near the bottom as one line to paste into the chat. You can also comment on this page. Click a picture to enlarge it.

Plan of the store, with camera numbers

One grid square is 128 map units; north is up, and you start in the arcade at the bottom. Upper floors are drawn darker and what lies below ground grey, each with its floor height; secrets are pink. The office is a balcony over the showroom's east end. Cameras 39 to 43 are in the night room, which no look touches.

Beyond paint: options to talk about

None of these is in the pictures. Each changes the level's shape, lighting or how it plays, so I would rather agree them first.

  1. Lights out. Indoors is evenly bright. After closing, the store could be dim with a pool of light at each lamp and a glow in every glass counter, as MAP07 is now; the shop windows and the office would stay lit. It makes the chaingunners on the gallery harder to see.
  2. A glass lantern. The middle of the atrium's roof is a square hole onto the stars. It could be stepped up and glazed, with the sky seen through glazing bars.
  3. Signs over the departments. TOYS, CHINA, LINEN and so on, lettered over each door in the look's own style. It would make the two wings easier to keep apart when carrying a door through them.
  4. Things on the floor. Mannequins, a rocking horse among the toys, a till on the cash office's desk, rolled carpets on end: new things to draw, as the telescope and the orrery were in MAP07. They would be sprites and stop nothing.
  5. The roof's edge. The roof is walled in 192 high. A low parapet with the town's roofs and the sky beyond would open it up, as the hill was opened in MAP07; its edges would be rebuilt.
  6. The arcade. It is a small square with shuttered shops on three sides. It could instead be a real arcade under a glass roof, or a street that runs on past the store in both directions.
  7. Room tints are gone in the looks, as in MAP01 to MAP07: the coloured column over each far side, the torches and the doors carry the colour.
  8. Mixing. The front, the lobby, the atrium, the showroom and each department are textured apart, so the atrium of one look with the departments of another costs nothing.
