Ajar · MAP32 "Double Shift" · look, as built

Deep Field, in the game

This is the look you picked, built into the five rooms and photographed by the level's tour. These are shots from the game, taken by a test run without a graphics card, so the lights are a little flatter than on your screen.

Stills cannot show what moves. The dashes on the conduits run, the high lines breathe, a wave of light crosses a room now and then, and here and there a line stutters. To see that, play it: MAP=MAP32 ./run.sh. The plain level is still there with AJAR_WORKS=0 ./build.sh.

Monsters stand in some shots: the later waves come even when the tour asks for none.

What I would look at first

  • Whether the ice-white lines are bright enough on your screen, and whether rooms 1, 2 and 5 are too dark.
  • Room 3 from the lobby: the sign over the press, the stars, the slabs.
  • The press: the hall dims as it warns and flares as it lands.
  • A room going white and steady once it is done.
  • Not built: roof beams under the stars in rooms 3 and 4. The sky there is open.
