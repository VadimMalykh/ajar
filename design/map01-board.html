To choose  The pit · 15 Nov 2026

Four treatments for the pit

Each changes the 192 units of wall below the hall floor, the pit floor and the light down there. The end walls now carry the hall's wall above floor level, and the tall dark column over the pit's frame is gone in all four. Shown from the same four cameras, with the pit as it is now for reference.

My pick is 2, the furnace. It is the only one that says "do not fall in" from the hall, it throws real light up the walls, and a furnace is one of the places the plan already wants doors to open onto. Option 1 is the safe choice if the pit should stay quiet. Decided: the hall wears terracotta and navy, which is why it appears in these shots, and the cell stays as it is.

Plan of the hall block, with camera numbers

One grid square is 64 map units. The cell, the vault and the way out are separate places reached only by door, so they are not on this plan.

What I see in these shots

  1. Everything is evenly bright. Light levels run from 170 to 210 out of 255. Nothing is dark, so nothing is lit, and light does not lead the eye to a door.
  2. Room tint colours everything. In the vault and the way out the tint washes over monsters, pickups and the door itself, so the door's own colour stands out less than it should.
  3. One wall panel does all the work. AJWALL repeats on walls up to 336 units tall with no skirting, cornice or break. Its dark band reads as a stripe painted round the room.
  4. Lamps do not light anything. Ceiling panels and torches are bright pictures of light. They cast nothing on the walls or floor near them.
  5. The dark trim reads as a hole. The near-black AJTRIM columns and pit walls look like missing geometry next to the pale walls.
  6. The pit does not look dangerous. A dark speckled floor between clean pale walls, lit almost as brightly as the hall.
  7. Doom's props are a second style. Torches, candelabra, barrels and tech lamps are detailed pixel art beside flat generated surfaces.
