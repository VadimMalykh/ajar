Not in these looks yet, to talk about

  1. Ceilings and lighting. Every look still has the grey ceiling panels and even light of the first build. Lamps light nothing. This is the same open point as in MAP01 and is better solved once for both levels.
  2. Room tints are gone in the looks. Upstairs was tinted warm, the attic gold and the cellar blue, after the door that leads there. As in MAP01, the looks leave that to the coloured column over each far side, the torches and the doors.
  3. Ground floor and upstairs are the same. They could differ, for example a plainer upstairs, so that you know which floor you are on.
  4. The door above the leaf. A room door shows a little of the hall's wall above it, also from inside the room. A deeper wooden surround hides most of it.
  5. Doom's props. Torches, candelabra and tech lamps are unchanged. The tech lamps on the balcony and in the garden fit none of the looks.
  6. The terrace and garden are not dressed at all yet. See their band above for what could be done.
