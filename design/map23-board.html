Sleeping Dogs: three looks for the animal house

The level wears MAP11's borrowed walls today and has no dressing. Here are three looks of its own, each an answer to one question: what did the institute take these animals for? Specimens, exhibits, or animals it kept and named.

Every look is built into the level twice, as the level is: THEN, by day, with the animals asleep in their runs, and NOW, the same walls forty years on with their bones. Nothing moves between the looks: only wall and floor textures, the pictures on the walls and a few things on the floor change, and the level plays and tests the same.

Pick a look for each row and paste the line near the bottom into the chat. Rows can be mixed freely; where two looks meet at a doorway, each side keeps its own. Click a picture to enlarge it.
