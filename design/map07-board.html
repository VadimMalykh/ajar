Small Hours: three looks for the observatory

The observatory still wears grey brick from cellar to dome. Below are three looks for the whole of it, each built into the level and photographed from the same 63 cameras as the level is now. A look changes textures only: no wall, floor or railing moves, and the level plays the same.

Picks can be mixed, for example the dome from one look and the rooms from another. Your picks are collected near the bottom as one line to paste into the chat. You can also comment on this page. Click a picture to enlarge it.

Beyond paint: options to talk about

None of these is in the pictures. Each changes the level's shape, lighting or how it plays, so I would rather agree them first.

  1. A telescope. The pier in the dome carries nothing. A telescope on it, pointing out of the slit, would be a new thing drawn for the level; it would stand above head height, so it blocks no shots from the floor and some from the gallery.
  2. A round dome. The dome is a square room. Cutting its corners to an octagon would make it read as a dome from inside and from the roof; the gallery, its frames and the fight would be rebuilt around that.
  3. Small hours. Indoors is evenly bright. Dim rooms with a pool of light at each lamp and case would suit all three looks and the level's name, and make the snipers on the stacks and the gallery harder to see.
  4. The hill. The wood stands as a wall 512 high on every side of the hillside and the knoll. A low wall with a horizon and stars beyond it would open the hill up; those edges would be rebuilt.
  5. The planetarium. It borrows the night room's walls and ceiling. It could have a projector in the middle and its own look, at the cost of the echo with the way out.
  6. Orrery and globe. The orrery room and the hall have glass cases where an orrery and a globe would stand. Both would be new things to draw.
  7. Room tints are gone in the looks, as in MAP01 to MAP06: the coloured column over each far side, the torches and the doors carry the colour.
  8. Mixing. Outside, hall, library, dome and rooms are textured apart, so the dome of one look with the rooms of another costs nothing.
