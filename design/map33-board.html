Third Person: three looks for the power station

The same station in three states: as it was built in the 1930s, as it was rebuilt in the 1960s, and long after its last coal. Each look is built into the level and photographed from the same 35 places. Nothing moves between the looks: only wall and floor textures, the pictures on the walls and the sky change, and the level plays and tests the same.

In every look the three parts keep their colours. The corridors keep their yellow, green and red light; the rooms are in white light now and carry a band of their part's colour along the walls instead. Nothing else in a look is yellow, green, red, blue, violet or pink.

Nothing is chosen yet: the level in the game is still the plain one, shown first in every row. The rows below are the parts of the station and can be mixed, for example the yards of one look and the switchgear hall of another. Your picks are collected near the bottom as one line to paste into the chat. Click a picture to enlarge it.

Plan of the station, with camera numbers

North is up; the level is about 9000 units wide. Part 1 (yellow) runs along the north, part 2 (green) through the middle, part 3 (red) along the south; all three end at their stands round the switchgear hall in the east. Cameras 31 to 35 are close views of the five pictures.
