The same in all four

  • The rooms, every distance and every puzzle thing stay where they are. Only textures, light and things you cannot bump into are added.
  • The time doors keep their ivory and their clock, the leaves their seven colours, the lamps red, amber and green.
  • Every leaf and frame stands in plain white light, so that its colour is never mixed with the room's.
  • Nothing warns of a paradox. The lights do not change near an earlier self.

What the game can really do

  • Lit textures that stay bright in a dark room, animated textures, the brightness and colour of each part of a room changed by script, and coloured lights that pulse and travel.
  • The soft glow in the sketches is a little more than the game will show: glow of that kind depends on your GZDoom settings, which I do not touch.
  • No mirror floors: your renderer may not draw them, so no concept leans on them.
  • Stills cannot show moving light. You will judge the built look by playing it from its worktree.
