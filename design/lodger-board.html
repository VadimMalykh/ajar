The Lodger: shadows

The first six looks were stiff figures built from rectangles, and beside Doom's own monsters they read as placeholders. This round changes how the lodger is made instead of only what it is.

Two ways are shown. In the first the lodger is the shadow of one of Doom's monsters: it borrows that monster's drawings, so it moves as well as they do and can be seen from every side. In the second it is a new figure of smoke, drawn soft and four times as fine as before. Your picks are collected at the bottom as one line to paste into the chat. You can also comment on this page. Click a picture to enlarge it.

A. The shadow of a Doom monster

Each is the monster's own animation filled with one flat near-black. In the frame it is faint, stepping out it thickens, and its corpse is a black stain. The four differ only in whose shadow it is.

B. How the shadow is drawn

The same revenant two ways. This choice is separate from whose shadow it is. A third way exists, the shimmer Doom uses for its spectre, but the renderer these photographs are taken with cannot show it, so it is only among the picks.

C. A figure of smoke

Not borrowed from anything: a soft dark shape that keeps the two brass hinge plates for eyes.

Worth knowing before you pick

  1. The Doom shadows have no eyes here. A borrowed shape is filled with one colour, so the brass hinge-plate eyes are gone. They can be put back as a small glowing pair that floats at head height, but that is extra work and I have not tried it yet.
  2. The lodger already sounds like a revenant. Its pain, death and hit sounds are the revenant's, picked when it was first built. With the revenant's shadow, sound and shape would agree.
  3. A borrowed shape ties the lodger to Doom II. That fits the game as it stands, a Doom II mod. If Ajar ever ships without Doom's files, only the smoke figure could come along.
  4. These are posed photographs. In the game the lodger still appears all at once in front of the frame and is still the old slab. Fading in and out of the frame is added once you choose.
