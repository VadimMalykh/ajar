Beyond paint: options to talk about

None of these is in the pictures. Each changes the level's shape, lighting or how it plays, so I would rather agree them first.

  1. Roofs against the sky. Every wall round the yard runs straight up to the sky's edge, so the houses have a painted top but no roof. Real eaves, with sky above a lower wall, need the yard's walls rebuilt in two steps. It would do most to stop the yard reading as a box.
  2. Marking the frames. With the dark column gone, the two frames in the yard are a dark doorway at ground level and nothing else. A stone surround or a lamp over each would mark them from across the yard, which matters with a thief about.
  3. Room tints are gone in the looks. Balcony, roof, loft and kennel were tinted after their doors. As in MAP01 to MAP03 the looks leave that to the coloured column over each far side, the torches and the doors. Patio brings a little of it back through the house paint; that could be done in the other two, or dropped.
  4. Evening. The yard is evenly bright under Doom's dusk sky. Darker, with the lit windows and the lamps doing the work, would suit the windows and the lodger. It makes the snipers on three sides harder to see.
  5. Things in the yard. A pump, washing lines, dustbins, a cart, chimneys on the roof. Each is a small block or a new sprite, and each changes where the thief and the monsters can walk.
  6. One house, or four. Each look gives the four sides four colours. All four the same is one line to change, and so is swapping which house has which colour.
  7. Mixing. Outside and inside are textured apart, so the fronts of one look with the rooms of another cost nothing.
