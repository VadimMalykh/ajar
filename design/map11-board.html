By Appointment: three looks for the way into the institute

MAP11 still wears the plain walls and tints it was built in. Below are three looks for the whole of it, each built into the level and photographed from the same 37 cameras as the level is now. A look changes textures and hangs pictures on walls: no wall, floor or thing moves, and the level plays the same.

This is the first level of the institute, so whatever is chosen here is where levels 12 to 20 start from.

Picks can be mixed, for example the lobby from one look and the labs from another. Your picks are collected near the bottom as one line to paste into the chat. You can also comment on this page. Click a picture to enlarge it.

Beyond paint: options to talk about

None of these is in the pictures. Each changes the level's shape, lighting or how it plays, so I would rather agree them first.

  1. Light. Every room keeps the even light it was built with. The corridor and the archive could be dim with a pool under each lamp, as MAP07 to MAP09 are; in Post Box some lamps could be out. The pump room is nearly black now, which hides its tanks and its spectres alike: a little more light, or one lamp over the gauges, would show the room without giving the spectres away.
  2. Windows. There are none. The lobby and the canteen could have tall windows onto the square MAP10's bus left you in. It would have to be settled what time of day it is there.
  3. Things on the floor. A potted palm in the lobby, chairs at the canteen's tables, an optical bench with a lamp in the lab, a trolley of files in the archive: sprites that stop nothing, as MAP08's dummies are.
  4. The machine. It is a pillar with four faces alike. It could be a frame standing free in the middle of the hall, dead, with its cables: the first thing in the game that looks made to hold a door. That says something about levels 21 to 30, so it wants deciding and not slipping in.
  5. The staff. Nobody is here. Coats on the pegs and cloths on the tables say they left in a hurry (Academy, Thaw) or long ago (Post Box). Which of the two it was is a story decision.
  6. The words. Every sign is English, as in the town, with Russian kept for the bus. The plates could carry a room number as well (OPTICS 104), which would also name the frames for the player.
  7. A railing. The gallery and the balcony have none. One with gaps at the two drops, as MAP09's gallery has, would show where the drops are.
  8. Room tints are gone in the looks, as in MAP01 to MAP09: the trim over each frame and far side and the doors carry the colour.
  9. Mixing. Each room is textured apart, and so are the furniture and the pictures, so the lobby of one look with the labs of another costs nothing.
