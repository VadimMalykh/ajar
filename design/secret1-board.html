This Is Where We Came In: three looks for the cinema

The secret level was built in plain grey. Below are three looks for the whole of it, each built into the level and photographed from the same 25 cameras as the plain level. A look changes textures, flats and the halls' light, and furnishes the foyer; nothing in a hall or a box is added or moves, and the level plays the same.

Look 1, Palace, is what the game wears until you choose, so that the level is dressed when you play it.

Beyond paint: options to talk about

None of these is in the pictures. Each changes the level's shape, lighting or how it plays, so they are better agreed first.

  1. Seats. The halls are bare floors with tall blocks. Rows of seats would have to be things (a hall may hold nothing that moves or changes but things), or low steps nobody trips on; either changes how the fights move.
  2. What the tall blocks are. Plinths, loudspeaker cabinets, banks of seats: one idea per look. They could be the same in all three, or something else 80 high.
  3. The screen. It is blank and hangs over the way in, because the north box sits where a projection box would. It could carry a still picture, or a lit white.
  4. Lights down. A cinema is dark. The halls are close to their first light; darker halls with lit boxes would look right and make the fights harder to read from a box.
  5. The time door. Ivory with a clock in every look. In look 2 it is close to the wall's white; it could be given a darker surround there.
  6. The posters. Six of the town's places. Others, or the same six redrawn per look, cost little.
  7. Signs. HALL 1 to 3, BOX OFFICE, CLOSED: English, as every sign of the town but the bus.
  8. The closet's corner. On the map the closet and the great hall's way in overlap by 32 (it was so before the looks). Nothing wrong shows in the pictures, but it has not been walked; it wants a look by hand.
