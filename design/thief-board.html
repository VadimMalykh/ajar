The thief: five looks and the one it has

The thief still wears its prototype sprite. Since the lodger ended up as a shadow of one of Doom's monsters, the five new candidates are all Doom's own sprites, made small and recoloured, with the door it has taken on its back. Each is posed in MAP04's yard, in the Tenement look, beside the north frame for scale.

Beyond the sprite: things to talk about

  1. The door on its back. In looks 2 to 6 it is a real door sprite in its own colour, three quarters of full size, so you can see across the yard which door it has. Now it is a plain slab. This can be taken with any look, including the present one.
  2. Size. All five are smaller than Doom's monsters, so it is told apart by height first. Say if it should be smaller still, or man-sized.
  3. A tell. It takes 35 tics at the hinges and makes no sound of its own while it works. A rattle or a creak then would let you hear a door going behind your back.
  4. Voice. It borrows the revenant's sight and death sounds, as the lodger does. A different pair from Doom's set costs nothing.
  5. When hurt. It has 40 health and never reacts to being shot. It could flinch, drop the door at half health, or run for the nearest hung door.
  6. Shown here, not built. These are posed photographs. The thief in the game is unchanged until you choose; walking, working and dying then get the chosen sprites' own frames.
