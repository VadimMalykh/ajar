The skill menu is headed "How long are you staying?" over your four names.

House Rules: "Notice to guests"

  1. A door remembers where it leads. Its colour and its sign say where.
  2. USE a door to take it down and carry it. One at a time.
  3. USE an empty frame to hang it there.
  4. Whatever can come through a door will. It works both ways.
  5. The way out has no colour.

Under them: "USE is Doom's open key. USE on a hung door while you carry one swaps the two; a carried door is put down only in a frame. Everything else is Doom's."

Quit messages

  • Leaving? Shut the door behind you.
  • Checking out so soon?
  • Leave it ajar and go?
  • The doors will keep. Go?
  • Nothing follows you out. Leave?
  • You can always come back the way you came. Quit?

Each is followed by "(Y to leave, N to stay)". The box itself stays in Doom's small red letters.
